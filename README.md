# Market Intelligence Agent

## Project Overview

This project evaluates whether a new smartphone brand should enter India’s ₹30,000–₹50,000 smartphone segment.

The analysis combines a smartphone product-listing dataset with external market research conducted through Amazon Quick Suite. It assesses market conditions, competition, product expectations, cost pressures, and barriers to entry.

## Recommendation

The recommendation is to pause a broad market entry under current conditions. A limited validation programme may be considered if the business can establish a differentiated product proposition, competitive component economics, credible distribution, and dependable after-sales service.

## Repository Structure

- `data/` contains the smartphone dataset and downloaded Quick Research summary.
- `screenshots/` contains evidence of the analysis, visualisations, sources, risks, and generated summary.
- `submission/` contains the final Market Intelligence Research Brief in Word and PDF format.

## Important Limitation

The smartphone dataset contains product listings and specifications. It is useful for reviewing visible features and price positioning, but it does not measure sales, market share, profitability, or actual customer demand.
